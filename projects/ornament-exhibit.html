Project

Ornament Re-composition

Year
2014
Category
Art direction · Graphic design
Role
Exhibition lead · Design
Scope
Branding · Exhibition design · Graphic design
Printer’s flowers and typographer’s garden

This curated exhibition investigated the aesthetic value of ornament typography in the modern era. The project encompassed the complete art direction and planning, including the visual identity for the public showcase in Insa-dong, Seoul. By presenting intricate digital compositions, the exhibition successfully brought the forgotten language of ornaments back into public discourse while celebrating the value of artistic immersion.
