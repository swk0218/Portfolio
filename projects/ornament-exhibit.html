Project

Ornament Re-composition

Year
2014
Category
Art direction · Graphic design
Role
Exhibition lead · Design
Scope
Branding · Exhibition design · Graphic design
Printer’s flowers and typographer’s garden

This curated exhibition investigated the aesthetic value of ornament typography in the modern era. The project encompassed the complete art direction and planning, including the visual identity for the public showcase in Insa-dong, Seoul. Through intricate digital compositions, the exhibition re-examines typographic ornaments as a subject of contemporary graphic design, inviting viewers to engage with their detail and decorative possibilities.
