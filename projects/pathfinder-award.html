2015 ADAA │ Semifinal
Deconstructing the illusion of authority in design

This independent award critiques the proliferation of design contests. By adopting an ornamental graphic style, the project exposes the illusion of authority through a satirical visual identity. It successfully engaged 454 entries from five countries on a minimal budget, deconstructing industry exclusivity by awarding all participants.
