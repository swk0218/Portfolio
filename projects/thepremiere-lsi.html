Distinct lifestyles, a shared sense of delight

Art direction for The Premiere 5, 7, and 9 translates each model’s price point, capabilities, and target audience into distinct lifestyle settings. Spaces, props, and atmosphere were tailored to product personas ranging from young families and couples with older children to affluent middle-aged couples and cinephiles. Together, the imagery differentiates each model while maintaining a delightful, family-oriented character across The Premiere lineup.
