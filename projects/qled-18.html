Full-stack brand design for the premium flagship launch

Shifting the focus from technical performance to holistic experience, this project establishes a Visual identity system for the QLED TV. By integrating premium and iconic assets with intuitive illustrations of core innovations, the system creates a cohesive brand narrative extending across photography, global retail environments, and packaging to make high-end technology more approachable.
