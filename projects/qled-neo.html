Project

Neo QLED

Year
2021
Category
Brand identity · Art direction
Role
Design · Art direction
Scope
Strategy · Branding · Photography
Vibrant evolution through fluid transparency.

This system evolves formal QLED brand foundations using a vibrant graphic palette and water-inspired visuals. Meticulous reflection effects are employed to symbolize crystal-clear picture quality. It successfully balances visual legacy with a refreshed, contemporary energy. The result is a modernized QLED brand language that redefines the aesthetic of premium display technology.
