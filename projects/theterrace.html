The Terrace

Year

Role

2021 iF │ Winner 2020 Good Design │ Winner
Bringing people together under the open sky

This visual identity system redefines the television experience by extending the warmth of home into vibrant outdoor environments. By moving away from conventional technical messaging, the visual language integrates inviting and natural concepts to focus on the joy of sharing moments with loved ones. It creates a distinctive identity suited for various settings such as terraces and patios, fostering deep emotional connections through an intuitive and organic aesthetic.
