Odyssey Series

Role

Orchestrating a cohesive dual identity

This visual identity expands the brand into a two-track strategy, seamlessly uniting the established lineups and the newly introduced lineups. To highlight their characteristics while maintaining a cohesive aesthetic, the system leverages a stark visual contrast: Neo’s signature dark backdrop against OLED’s luminous environment. Anchored by the shared 'Core' lighting motif, this adaptable framework solidifies a multi-dimensional presence in the gaming market.
