Carry your experience in style

The visual branding for The Movingstyle captures the free and versatile usability of a portable screen. To establish distinct market differentiation, the identity is built around a unique persona, highlighting the product’s iconic qualities through vibrant colors. Supported by a flexible visual system for adaptation across diverse media, it incorporates linear graphic assets that evoke constant movement, intuitively expressing the dynamic essence of the product.
