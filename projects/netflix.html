Netflix Korean titles

Year
2015
Category
Graphic design
Role
Design
Scope
Graphic design · Lettering
Localizing global content into Korean

This project translates the narrative and visual character of original English title lettering into Korean. Adapting alphabetic letterforms to Hangul’s syllable-block structure required balancing the originals’ distinctive shapes, textures, and composition with legibility and readability in Korean. The lettering preserves the stories and cinematic atmosphere embedded in the original titles for Korean audiences.
