Project

Wireless Audio VL

Year
2017
Category
Brand identity
Role
Design
Scope
Strategy · Branding · Packaging
2018 IDEA │ Gold 2019 iF │ Winner
A rhythmic visual evolution redefining the emotive sound experience.

Derived from the product’s geometric form, repeating graphic motifs create a visual rhythm for the VL Series. Bold typography and strong color contrasts convey the intended impression of powerful sound, while keeping the product’s simple geometry central to the design. These elements form a visual identity system that connects product form with sound performance.
