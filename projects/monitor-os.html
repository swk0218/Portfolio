Screen identity
A definitive image series for the Odyssey gaming lineup

This project defines the screen identity for the Odyssey gaming monitor series. Developed in collaboration with concept artist Jan Urschel, the image series translates the "Odyssey" name into a visual saga of venturing into the unknown. Over the course of three years, the project established a cohesive identity capturing the entire narrative arc of an interstellar expedition. This journey encompasses every stage from the vessel’s launch to its deep-space voyage and ultimate discovery. The aesthetic pursues a minimal futurism that aligns with Odyssey’s overarching brand language.
