Project

The Premiere 8K

Year
2024
Category
Brand identity · Art direction
Role
Design · Art direction
Scope
Strategy · Branding · Guidelines · Photography
Crafting an ultra-premium visual narrative

Breaking away from feature-driven marketing, this project positions The Premiere 8K as an ultra-premium luxury lifestyle product. Guided by the concept of "Between Hyper Reality and Still Life," the visual identity utilizes a proportion-based grid system and image-centric principles. Luxury furniture-style photography elegantly highlights the projector's exquisite craftsmanship and materials without distraction.
